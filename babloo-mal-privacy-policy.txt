Privacy Policy for babloo - malayalam
Last Updated: January 06, 2026

Babloo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our youngest users. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information through our mobile application.

1. No Data Collection
Babloo is an Offline-First application.

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from our users.

We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or usage statistics.

Everything you do within the app stays on your device.

2. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
Babloo is specifically designed for children under the age of 13 (toddlers).

Because we collect no personal data, we are fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

There are no social media integrations, no behavioral tracking, and no hidden data mining.

3. Local Storage: Photos and Voice Recordings
The Premium version of Babloo allows parents to record their own voices and upload family photos to personalize the learning experience.

Local Only: All audio recordings and photos are stored locally on your device's internal storage.

No Cloud Uploads: These files are never uploaded to our servers, shared with third parties, or accessible by the developer.

Deletion: Deleting the app will permanently remove all locally stored custom content.

4. Permissions
Babloo may request the following permissions for core functionality:

Microphone: Required only if the parent chooses to record their voice for the "Custom Audio" feature.

Storage/Gallery: Required only if the parent chooses to select a photo for the "Relationships" cards.

In-App Purchases: Used to facilitate the one-time yearly subscription via the Google Play Billing system.

5. Third-Party Services
We use Google Play Billing to handle subscriptions. When you make a purchase, Google handles your payment information securely. We do not see or store your credit card or payment details.

6. No Advertising
To ensure a safe and focused environment for toddlers, Babloo contains zero third-party advertisements.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect your email, we encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.

8. Contact Us
